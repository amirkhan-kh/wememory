# WAMemory — Privacy Policy (draft)

**Status:** draft for review · not legal advice  
**Languages:** publish EN + RU on the website/app store

## Summary
WAMemory helps users keep a local archive of WhatsApp message text that appears in Android notifications. We are not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## What we collect
- Account email and authentication data (when you register)
- Subscription / payment status via a payment provider (we do not store full card numbers)
- On-device message archive created from notification content you grant access to
- Basic diagnostics (crashes, performance) — without message body in production logs

## How archive works
- On Android, with Notification Access enabled, WAMemory may save title/body text from WhatsApp notifications.
- Media may be limited by what the notification exposes.
- Deleted WhatsApp messages are not “restored” into WhatsApp; only the copy already saved in WAMemory can be shown.

## Storage
- Message archive is stored primarily on the user’s device (SQLite).
- Cloud sync/backup (if enabled later) will be optional and described before activation.
- Retention and storage quotas follow the active subscription plan.

## Sharing
- We do not sell message content.
- Support staff do not read private chats by default.
- We may share account/billing data with payment processors and as required by law.

## User controls
- Revoke Notification Access anytime in Android settings (archiving stops).
- Delete account / local data (feature to be shipped before public launch).
- Export archive (planned).

## Children’s privacy
Service is not directed to children under 13 (or higher age required in your market).
