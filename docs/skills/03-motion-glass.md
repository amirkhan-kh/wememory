# 03 motion-glass

WAMemory visual skill (replaces TradeFlow landing aurora notes).

## Do

- Glass tiles: blur 8–18, fill white 6–12% or panel translucent, 1px light border, top specular.
- Surreal bg: teal `#1DE0C4` + violet `#7C6CFF` soft radials on `#070B10` — slow opacity/transform drift only.
- Scroll 3D: perspective `Matrix4`, small `rotateX`, scale settle; clamp angles.
- Brand green `#00A884` / `#25D366` remains primary CTA/unread — violet only as mist.

## Don't

- Full-screen stacked blurs (jank).
- Galaxy stars/planets by default.
- TradeFlow navy `#32536b` / sky `#9cc3dc` CRM palette.
- Dizzy >12° tilts or parallax that hides unread badges.

## Check

- Reduced motion → flat list.
- Manual scroll on chat list feels premium, not sick.
